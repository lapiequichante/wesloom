# 74. Rebase each frame onto an f64 floating origin

Date: 2026-10-13

Status: Accepted

## Context

`wxsl_relative_to_eye` (ADR 0008's amendment, landed with ADR 0026) made
`world_position` camera-relative in the shader, but bought no precision:
by the time a position reached the GPU it was an absolute `f32`, which has
a whole unit between neighbours at 10^7. plan3 M10 is the host half —
model matrices pre-translated by the camera in `f64`, and no camera
translation left in the view matrix.

The flag is per *material*, while the instance rows, the camera, the
lights and the shadow matrices are per *frame*. Rebasing the frame for
flagged materials alone would leave every other material reading a
shifted world.

## Decision

The frame is rebased, not the material:

* `Environment::origin` (`DVec3`, default zero) is where the frame's
  coordinates are measured from. The camera, the previous camera, the
  lights and every draw transform are relative to it; the shadow views
  follow because they are computed from those.
* The host does the `f64` work: `Environment::look_from(eye, target)` puts
  the origin on the eye (so the view matrix has no translation),
  `local_transform` pre-translates a `DMat4` model, `local_point` a
  position. All of it is in `wxsl-frame`, shared with every backend.
* The scene uniform carries the origin's `f32` copy (`Scene.origin`,
  generated by `wxsl_core::host`, ADR 0050). `world_origin()` — "where
  `world_position` is measured from, in buffer space" — is `camera.position`
  under the flag and `-scene.origin` without it, so an unflagged material
  still reads the absolute world, and `world_position + world_origin()`
  is still buffer space, where lights and shadow matrices live.
* The deferred lighting pass measures its depth-reconstructed position
  from `world_origin()` like the forward path does. It used to treat the
  buffer-space position as the material's frame, which was wrong under
  the flag before any rebasing.

## Alternatives considered

* **Rebase only when every material is flagged.** A frame-wide mode
  gated on a per-material flag fails silently the first time one
  material is not flagged.
* **`f64` everywhere on the host (`Camera::eye: DVec3`, `DrawItem` with a
  `DMat4`).** Every call site of the two most used types would change for
  the benefit of the few scenes that are large; an origin plus three
  helpers costs one field.

## Consequences

* Geometry never jitters under a rebased frame, flagged or not. An
  unflagged material at 10^7 still pays in its *shading*: its
  `world_position` is an absolute `f32`, and `view_direction` cancels two
  large numbers. That is the honest price of asking for absolute space.
* An application that rebases supplies previous-frame transforms and the
  previous camera against *this* frame's origin (through the same
  helpers), or the velocity stage sees the origin's motion as the scene's.
* `Scene` grew 16 bytes; the Dawn header is regenerated, and a C++ host
  that zero-fills the uniform gets today's behaviour.
* `frame_of_reference.rs` holds both halves of M10's acceptance test:
  near the origin the flag changes no pixel, and a plane at 10^7 rebased
  onto the eye renders as it does at the origin under forward and
  deferred, while the naive `f32` frame is wrecked.
